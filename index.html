Reading the numbers

  • Not a medical device. Camera pulse extraction is sensitive to motion, lighting and skin tone. Treat it as a signal source, not a diagnostic.
  • Give it ~10 s. The heart-rate estimate is spectral over a 10 s window, so it needs that much history before it locks.
  • Confidence is the gate. Below ~0.55 the state stays searching — drive your visuals from confidence so they degrade honestly rather than animating noise.
  • Steady, diffuse light beats bright light. Avoid a screen behind you, flickering LEDs, and anything that makes the camera hunt for exposure.
  • HRV from a webcam is indicative only. RMSSD needs beat timing far more precise than 30 fps gives you.
